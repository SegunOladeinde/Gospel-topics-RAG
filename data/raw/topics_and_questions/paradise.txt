“Paradise,” Topics and Questions (2023)

Overview

Paradise

Paradise is the part of the spirit world in which the righteous spirits who have departed from this life await the Resurrection. It is a condition of happiness and peace.

In the scriptures , the word paradise is used in different ways. First, as mentioned above, it designates a place of peace and happiness in the postmortal spirit world , reserved for those who have been baptized and who have remained faithful. 1 Those in spirit prison have the opportunity to learn the gospel of Jesus Christ , repent of their sins, and receive the ordinances of baptism and confirmation through the work we do in temples . 2 If they accept the gospel and their temple work has been done, they may enter paradise.

A second use of the word paradise is found in Luke’s account of the Savior’s Crucifixion. When Jesus was on the cross, a thief who also was being crucified said, “Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.” 3 The Lord replied, “Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise.” 4 The Prophet Joseph Smith explained that this is a mistranslation; the Lord actually said that the thief would be with Him in the world of spirits.

The word paradise also sometimes refers to the celestial kingdom . 5 In the tenth article of faith, the word paradisiacal describes the earth’s glory in the Millennium .
