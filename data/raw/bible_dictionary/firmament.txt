Firmament

The expanse of the heavens. It is the KJV English translation of a Hebrew word meaning expanse, and is spoken of in Gen. 1 ; Ps. 19:1 ; Moses 2 ; Abr. 4; Abr., fac. 1, fig. 12 ; see esp. Abr. 4:7–8, 20 .
