Lazarus

Helped of God. (1) Of Bethany, brother of Martha and Mary ( John 11:1–44 ; 12:1–2, 9–11 ); raised by Jesus from the dead.

(2) Of the parable ( Luke 16:19–25 ); the meaning of the name may have suggested its use here.
