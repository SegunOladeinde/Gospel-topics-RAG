Elath or Eloth

A town of Edom, on the Gulf of Aqabah, one of the “stations” of the Israelites in the wilderness ( Deut. 2:8 ); a naval station during the monarchy ( 1 Kgs. 9:26 ; 2 Kgs. 8:20 ; 14:22 ; 16:6 ).
