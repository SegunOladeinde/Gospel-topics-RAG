Paran, Wilderness of

The desert between Judea and Sinai, forming the central part of the Sinaitic peninsula ( Gen. 21:21 ; Num. 10:12 ; 12:16 ; 13:3, 26 ; Deut. 1:1 ; 33:2 ; 1 Sam. 25:1 ; 1 Kgs. 11:18 ; Hab. 3:3 ); called El-paran ( Gen. 14:6 ).
