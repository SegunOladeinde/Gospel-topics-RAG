Rehoboth

Broad places. (1) A city built by Nimrod, probably the northeastern suburb of Nineveh ( Gen. 10:11 ).

(2) A well dug by Isaac ( Gen. 26:22 ).

(3) A town on the Euphrates ( Gen. 36:37 ; 1 Chr. 1:48 ).
