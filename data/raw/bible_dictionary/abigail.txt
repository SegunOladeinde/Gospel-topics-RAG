Abigail

Father of rejoicing. (1) Wife of Nabal ( 1 Sam. 25:3 ); became David’s wife ( 25:42 ; 27:3 ; 30:5 ; 2 Sam. 2:2 ); mother of Chileab ( 2 Sam. 3:3 ), or Daniel ( 1 Chr. 3:1 ).

(2) Sister of David ( 1 Chr. 2:16 ).
