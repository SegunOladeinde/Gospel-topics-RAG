Peniel

The Face of God ( Gen. 32:30 ). Elsewhere, Penuel. (1) By the Jordan, where Jacob wrestled with a man ( Gen. 32:31 ); the town there was destroyed by Gideon ( Judg. 8:8–9, 17 ); rebuilt by Jeroboam ( 1 Kgs. 12:25 ).

(2) 1 Chr. 4:4 .

(3) 1 Chr. 8:25 .
