Claudius

(1) Emperor of Rome, from A.D. 41 to 51 ( Acts 11:28 ; 18:2 ).

(2) Claudius Lysias, the Roman military tribune in Jerusalem, who rescued Paul and sent him for trial to Caesarea ( Acts 21:31–40 ; 22:24 ; 23:10, 26, 30 ; 24:7 ).
