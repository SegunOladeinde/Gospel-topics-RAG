Turtle

The turtledove, as in Song of Solomon: “the voice of the turtle is heard in our land,” being a sign of springtime ( 2:11–13 ; see also Jer. 8:7 ). Turtledoves were used for offerings, as in Gen. 15:9 ; Lev. 1:14 ; 5:7, 11 ; 12:6–8 ; 14:22, 30 ; 15:29 ; Num. 6:10 ; Luke 2:24 .
