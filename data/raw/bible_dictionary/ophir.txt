Ophir

(1) Son of Joktan ( Gen. 10:29 ; 1 Chr. 1:23 ).

(2) A country whence gold was brought, probably a port of southern Arabia ( 1 Kgs. 9:28 ; 10:11 ; 22:48 ; 1 Chr. 29:4 ; 2 Chr. 8:18 ; 9:10 ; Job 22:24 ; 28:16 ; Ps. 45:9 ; Isa. 13:12 ).
