Shushan

A lily. Also known as Susa; formerly the capital of Elam; under Cyrus the capital of the Persian Empire ( Neh. 1:1 ; Esth. 1:2, 5 ; 2:3, 5, 8 ; 3:15 ; 4:16 ; 8:14–15 ; 9:6–18 ); capital of Elam ( Dan. 8:2 ).
