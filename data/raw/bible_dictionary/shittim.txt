Shittim

Acacias. The district east of Jordan opposite Jericho, where the people encamped before crossing the Jordan ( Num. 25:1 [Abelshittim, Num. 33:49 ]; Josh. 2:1 ; 3:1 ; Micah 6:5 ); “shall water the valley of Shittim” ( Joel 3:18 ).
