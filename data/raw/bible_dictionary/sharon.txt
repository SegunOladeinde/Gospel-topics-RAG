Sharon

The maritime plain, about 10 miles wide and 50 miles in length, extending from Carmel to Joppa, celebrated for its beauty and its flowers ( 1 Chr. 5:16 ; 27:29 ; Song 2:1 ; Isa. 33:9 ; 35:2 ; 65:10 ; Acts 9:35 ).
