Zin

The site is unknown, but the use of the name “wilderness of Zin” shows it must have been near Kadesh-barnea in the extreme south of Palestine ( Num. 13:21 ; 20:1 ; 27:14 ; 33:36 ; 34:3–4 ; Deut. 32:51 ; Josh. 15:1, 3 ).
