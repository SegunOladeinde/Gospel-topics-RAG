Juda

(1) Son of Joanna or Joanan ( Luke 3:27 ).

(2) Mentioned in our Lord’s genealogy ( Luke 3:30 ).

(3) Patriarch Judah ( Matt. 2:6 and elsewhere in the New Testament).

(4) One of the Lord’s “brethren” ( Mark 6:3 ).
