Artaxerxes

Longimanus. Son of Xerxes and king of Persia, 465–425 B.C. ; hinders Jews from building ( Ezra 4:7–23 ); gives commission to Ezra ( 6:14 ; 7:1–21 ; 8:1 ) and to Nehemiah ( Neh. 2:1 ; 5:14 ; 13:6 ).
