Michal

Saul’s second daughter, wife of David ( 1 Sam. 14:49 ; 18:20, 27 ; 19:11–13, 17 ; 25:44 ; 2 Sam. 3:13–16 ; 6:16, 23 ). In 2 Sam. 21:8 the name Michal is mistakenly used for Merab, who was the wife of Adriel.
