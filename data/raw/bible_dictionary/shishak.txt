Shishak

King of Egypt, being the first of the 22nd dynasty ( 1 Kgs. 11:40 ; 14:25–26 ; 2 Chr. 12:2–9 ). A pictorial representation of his victory over Rehoboam has been discovered on the wall of the temple of Karnak.
